Project Details

Shiksha Setu

The Challenge

Understanding the Issue

In rural West Bengal, thousands of children between the ages of 6 and 14 are either out of school or attend overcrowded government schools with minimal resources. The student-to-teacher ratio often exceeds 60:1, making quality education impossible.

Many families depend on daily-wage labour, and children — especially girls — are often pulled out of school to help with household duties or agricultural work. Without intervention, these children lose their chance at a better future.

In the villages we surveyed, only 38% of children aged 10-14 could read a simple paragraph in their mother tongue. This is the crisis Shiksha Setu was born to address.

What We Do

Our Work & Activities

Shiksha Setu takes a holistic approach — combining physical learning centres, digital tools, teacher training, and community engagement to create lasting educational change.

Community Learning Centres

We have established 12 community-run learning centres in remote villages, each equipped with books, stationery, and basic furniture. These centres serve as after-school hubs where children receive remedial education and homework support.

Digital Classrooms

Tablet-based smart classrooms with pre-loaded educational content in Bengali and English. Interactive lessons in maths, science, and language make learning engaging. Solar panels ensure these work even without grid electricity.

Teacher Training Programmes

Quarterly workshops for local teachers on modern pedagogical methods, activity-based learning, and inclusive education practices. We have trained 45 teachers across 18 villages so far.

Girl Child Enrollment Drives

Door-to-door campaigns and community meetings to bring out-of-school girls back to education. We provide free uniforms, books, and menstrual hygiene kits to remove barriers.

Mid-Day Meal Support

Nutritious mid-day meals at every learning centre, ensuring children don't have to choose between food and education. Our meals are designed by nutritionists for growing children.

Parent & Community Engagement

Monthly parent-teacher meetings and community awareness programmes on the value of education. We work with village panchayats to build local ownership of the programme.
